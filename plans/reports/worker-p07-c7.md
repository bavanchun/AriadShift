The engine-neutral `ariad_core::ocr` module defines `OcrPage`, `OcrParagraph`, `OcrLine`, `OcrBaseline`, `OcrWord`, `OcrBox`, `OcrOrientation`, the three size constants and `OcrPage::validate`. Validation checks image geometry, boxes, NFC text, confidence, finite baselines, word count and serialized size. Serialization is counted without allocating a second result buffer. Parameter modules provide the six public option types and the three internal option types; `ocr_scan::specs()` and `internal_specs()` describe the approved public and internal operations. The specifications are not registered in the global tool registry until the engines and host routing exist. Internal render emits `pNNNN.png` and `scan.json` with page, dimensions, DPI, skew and clean state; recognition emits `<n>.ocr.json` and orientation emits `<n>.osd.json`.

Four generated PDFs and truth companions are in `fixtures/pdf-tools/`. `scan-photos-3.pdf` is 46,365 bytes and records three bounding boxes and angles; `scan-rotated-90-vi.pdf` is 63,234 bytes and records clockwise correction 270; `scan-mixed-text-vi.pdf` is 125,174 bytes and records visible and invisible text by page; `scan-dirty-vi.pdf` is 123,443 bytes and records background, speckles and border. The generator was run twice with identical PDF SHA-256 values. PDF inspection confirmed three mixed pages, their text rendering modes, and zero page rotation on the rotated-image fixture. Hashes and source attribution are in the fixture manifest.
