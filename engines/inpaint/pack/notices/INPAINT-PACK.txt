AriadShift inpaint development engine pack

Engine adapter: Apache-2.0 AND MIT (repository LICENSE and NOTICE).
Development runtime: host Python, PSF-2.0. The release runtime is pinned to
python-build-standalone 3.14.9+20261021 in pins.toml; development builds do not
claim to contain that standalone archive.
PDF parser: unmodified pypdf 7.0.0, BSD-3-Clause (pypdf-BSD-3-Clause.txt).
No native wheels or model weights are included in the development pack.
Florence-2-base configuration: MIT (Florence-2-MIT.txt).
Optional OpenCV Telea/NS: Apache-2.0 (OpenCV-Apache-2.0.txt).
The portable detector uses neutral gray connected components. Florence weights
are not bundled or loaded. Pure Python uses fast marching or biharmonic filling.
LaMa (big-lama) and Places365 weights are excluded pending owner licence review.
