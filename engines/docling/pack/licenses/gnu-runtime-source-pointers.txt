not established: their .comment sections were stripped. The ABI series below
is the earliest upstream release series evidenced by the exported versioned
interfaces, not a claim that these exact binaries were built from that release.
Backports and vendor builds can preserve or introduce the same interfaces.
Imported GCC or GLIBC version requirements identify required runtime ABIs;
they do not identify the GCC source release of the importing library.

actual redistributed objects, including any provider changes and build scripts.
A compatible upstream ABI baseline alone is not corresponding-source proof.
